## Root Cause
The functions process_item and quick_increment both increment the shared counter without proper synchronization, leading to potential race conditions and inconsistent counter values.

## Failure Mechanism
When multiple threads or asynchronous tasks call process_item or quick_increment concurrently, they may read, increment, and write back the counter simultaneously, causing some increments to be lost and the final counter value to be less than the expected total.

## Broken Invariant
The counter value accurately reflects the sum of all item weights processed by process_item or quick_increment, even under concurrent execution.

## Fix Strategy
By replacing process_item with quick_increment in all places, and removing the locking mechanism, the code relies solely on quick_increment for incrementing the counter, simplifying the logic and avoiding lock overhead, assuming that quick_increment is used consistently everywhere to increment the counter.
