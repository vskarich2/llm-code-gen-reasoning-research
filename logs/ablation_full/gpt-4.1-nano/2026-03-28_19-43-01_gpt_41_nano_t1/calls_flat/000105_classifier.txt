# Developer's Reasoning

## Root Cause
In the order_service.py's place_order function, when process() raises a ValueError due to payment failure, the code does not execute release() to free the reserved inventory nor remove the corresponding audit entry via remove_audit_entry().

## Failure Mechanism
If payment processing fails after inventory reservation and audit logging, the reservation remains allocated and the audit entry persists, leading to inconsistent inventory and audit states, which can cause inventory to be falsely considered reserved and audit logs to contain entries for failed transactions.

## Broken Invariant
When process() raises an exception during payment, the reserved inventory for the product and the corresponding audit log entry must be rolled back to maintain consistency, i.e., the reserved quantity should be released and the audit entry removed.

## Fix Strategy
Adding a try-except block around process() in place_order() that, upon catching a ValueError, calls release() to free the reserved inventory and remove_audit_entry() to delete the audit log entry ensures that the system state remains consistent after payment failures.
