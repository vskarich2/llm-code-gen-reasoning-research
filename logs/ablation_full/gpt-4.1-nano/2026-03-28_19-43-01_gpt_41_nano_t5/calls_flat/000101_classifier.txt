# Developer's Reasoning

## Root Cause
In the order_service.py, the place_order function calls reserve() to reserve inventory but does not call release() to undo the reservation if the payment process fails, specifically when _process_payment raises a ValueError.

## Failure Mechanism
When a payment failure occurs during place_order, the inventory remains reserved because the exception is re-raised without releasing the reservation, leading to inventory being stuck as reserved after a payment failure.

## Broken Invariant
After a payment failure during place_order, the reserved inventory for the product should be released, ensuring that available inventory reflects the actual stock and no reservations are left hanging due to failed payments.

## Fix Strategy
Adding a finally block in place_order that calls release() to free the reserved inventory if an exception occurs during payment processing ensures that reservations are properly cleaned up after failures, maintaining the invariant that reservations are only held for successful payments.

    """Place an order: reserve inventory, process payment, notify.

    return {"status": "confirmed", "payment": result}
