# Developer's Reasoning

## Root Cause
The charge() function in payment.py returns early when a cached transaction exists, skipping the call to log_charge().

## Failure Mechanism
When charge() is called with a txn_id that is already in _charge_cache, it returns the cached result without executing log_charge(), resulting in missing audit log entries for cached charges.

## Broken Invariant
Every call to charge(), whether it results in a cache hit or a new charge, must invoke log_charge() to record the event in the audit log.

## Fix Strategy
Moving the log_charge() call outside the cache check ensures that every invocation of charge() logs the charge event, regardless of whether it was cached or newly created.
