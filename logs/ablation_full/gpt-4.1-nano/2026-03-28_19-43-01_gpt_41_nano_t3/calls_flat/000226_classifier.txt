## Root Cause

This inconsistent lock ordering causes a deadlock when two transfers are interleaved, with one holding A and waiting for B, and the other holding B and waiting for A.

## Failure Mechanism

If both transfers acquire their first lock before attempting the second, each transfer holds one resource and waits for the other, resulting in a deadlock scenario where neither can proceed.

## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy
