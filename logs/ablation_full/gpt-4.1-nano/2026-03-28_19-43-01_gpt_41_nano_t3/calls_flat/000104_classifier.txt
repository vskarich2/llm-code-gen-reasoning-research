## Root Cause
In the order_service.py's place_order function, when process() raises a ValueError due to payment failure, the code does not execute release() to free the reserved inventory or remove_audit_entry() to delete the audit log entry, leaving the reservation and audit log inconsistent with the actual payment status.

## Failure Mechanism

## Broken Invariant
The reservation of inventory and the audit log entry must be rolled back if payment processing fails, ensuring that no inventory remains reserved and no audit entries exist for failed payments.

## Fix Strategy
Adding a try-except block around process() in place_order() that, upon catching a ValueError, calls release() to free the reserved inventory and remove_audit_entry() to delete the audit log entry, restores consistency by rolling back partial state changes after a payment failure.
