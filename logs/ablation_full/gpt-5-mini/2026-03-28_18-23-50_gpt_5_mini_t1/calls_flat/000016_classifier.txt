    Behavior and rules:
    - If 'name' is present in changes, set both user['name'] and user['display_name'] to that value.
    - If only 'display_name' is present, treat it as an alias for 'name' and update both fields.

            changes_copy[k] = v

    if "display_name" in changes_copy and "name" not in changes_copy:
        changes_copy["name"] = changes_copy["display_name"]
