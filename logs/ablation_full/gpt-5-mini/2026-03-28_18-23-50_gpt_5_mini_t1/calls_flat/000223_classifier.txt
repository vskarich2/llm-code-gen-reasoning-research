## Failure Mechanism

## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy

This breaks the causal chain because the lost-item step (error + early return) is removed and replaced by a safe retention + later processing step. The observable effect is that items arriving before init() are processed when init() runs and thus the invariant is restored.
