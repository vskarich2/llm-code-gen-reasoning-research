## Failure Mechanism

## Broken Invariant
After any call to insert_column, len(self.headers) == len(self.column_widths) must hold. Currently this is violated because self.column_widths is not updated to include the new column width.

## Fix Strategy

Use the header's length to initialize the new column's width: self.column_widths.insert(position, len(header)). This directly restores the invariant len(self.headers) == len(self.column_widths) and ensures format_table can safely index widths by column. This is the minimal, local change to break the causal chain (missing widths insertion) without changing other behaviors.

        self.rows = []
        self.column_widths = [default_width] * len(headers)
