As a result, _reserved in the inventory module and entries in payment._audit_log remain changed after a payment failure.

## Failure Mechanism
Concrete scenario: payment.set_gateway_fail(True) causes payment.process to raise ValueError. place_order first calls reserve(product_id, qty) (which increments inventory._reserved[product_id]) and then add_audit_entry(...) The effect is that inventory.get_reserved(product_id) remains incremented by qty (reducing available stock) and payment.get_audit_log() still contains the orphaned order entry even though the payment failed.

## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy

Also make reset() delegate to the inventory and payment modules' reset() functions to avoid leaving stale module-local state. These changes ensure reservation and audit log are undone when payment fails.
