## Root Cause
The function 'update_product' in 'api.py' calls both 'write_fresh' and 'write_cached', leading to redundant writes of product data. This redundancy can cause stale data to be returned if 'write_cached' is called after 'write_fresh' without a new computation.

## Failure Mechanism
For example, if 'update_product' is called with a product ID and a compute function, 'write_fresh' computes a new value and updates the version, but then 'write_cached' may write an old cached value without updating the version, resulting in the product data being stale.

## Broken Invariant
The invariant that the product data should always reflect the most recent computation with an updated version is violated, as 'write_cached' can overwrite the fresh write without a version update.

## Fix Strategy
By removing the call to 'write_cached' in 'update_product', we ensure that only the fresh computation is written, maintaining the integrity of the versioning and preventing stale data from being returned.
