## Root Cause
The bug is in the execute_transfer function, where multiple logging calls (record_transfer_attempt, emit_failure_alert, emit_transfer_event) are mixed with the core business logic of transferring funds between accounts. This leads to a lack of separation of concerns.

## Failure Mechanism
In a scenario where a transfer is attempted, if a transient failure occurs during the credit phase, the function raises a RuntimeError after logging the failure. This can lead to inconsistent states where the debit is recorded but the credit fails, violating the expected behavior of a complete transfer.

## Broken Invariant
The invariant that a transfer must either fully succeed or fully fail is violated. Specifically, if the debit is recorded but the credit fails, the sender's balance is reduced while the receiver's balance remains unchanged, leading to an inconsistent state.

## Fix Strategy
By extracting the core transfer logic into a helper function and moving all observability calls into a wrapper, the code ensures that the transfer logic is clean and focused solely on the business operation. This separation allows for better handling of success and failure scenarios without mixing in logging, thus maintaining the invariant.
