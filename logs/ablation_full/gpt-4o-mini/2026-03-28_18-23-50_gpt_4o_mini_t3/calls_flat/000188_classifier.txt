# Developer's Reasoning

## Root Cause
The bug is in the execute_transfer function, where multiple logging calls (record_transfer_attempt, emit_failure_alert, emit_transfer_event) are mixed with the core business logic of transferring funds between accounts. This leads to a lack of separation of concerns.

## Failure Mechanism
When a transfer is executed, if a transient failure occurs during the credit phase, the function emits a failure alert and raises an exception. This can lead to inconsistent states where the debit is recorded but the credit fails, violating the expected behavior of a complete transfer.

## Broken Invariant
The invariant that a transfer must either fully succeed (both debit and credit) or fully fail is violated. If the credit fails after the debit has been recorded, the sender's balance is reduced while the receiver's balance remains unchanged.

## Fix Strategy
By extracting the core transfer logic into a helper function and moving all observability calls into a wrapper, the code ensures that the transfer logic is clean and isolated. This change allows for better handling of success and failure scenarios without mixing business logic with logging.
