## Root Cause

## Failure Mechanism
When a user is saved, both functions are called separately, which can lead to discrepancies if one function is updated and the other is not. For example, if 'sync_user_to_cache' is modified to include additional data but 'refresh_user_snapshot' is not, the cache may not reflect the most current user information.

## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy
By consolidating 'sync_user_to_cache' and 'refresh_user_snapshot' into a single function, we ensure that all user caching operations are handled uniformly. This change eliminates redundancy and reduces the risk of inconsistencies in the cache.
