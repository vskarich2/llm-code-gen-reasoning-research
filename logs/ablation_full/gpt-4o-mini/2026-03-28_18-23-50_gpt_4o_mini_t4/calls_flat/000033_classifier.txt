## Root Cause
The bug is in the `update_product` function in `catalog.py`, specifically the lack of a call to `invalidate_local(product_id)` after updating the product. This means that the local cache remains stale even after the product data is updated in the database.

## Failure Mechanism
When a product is updated using `update_product`, the local cache is not invalidated. As a result, if `get_product` is called immediately after an update, it may return stale data from the local cache instead of fetching the updated data from the database.

## Broken Invariant
After updating a product, all cache layers must be invalidated to ensure that subsequent reads return the most current data. This invariant is violated because the local cache is not cleared, allowing stale data to persist.

## Fix Strategy
By adding a call to `invalidate_local(product_id)` in the `update_product` function, the local cache will be cleared whenever a product is updated. This ensures that the next call to `get_product` retrieves the updated data from the database instead of stale data from the local cache.
