## Root Cause

## Failure Mechanism
When a charge is made with a transaction ID that has already been processed, the function checks '_charge_cache' and finds the ID. It then returns the cached result without logging the charge, leading to an incomplete audit log. ## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy
The fix involves adding a call to 'log_charge()' before returning the cached result in the 'charge' function. This ensures that every charge attempt, including those that hit the cache, is logged appropriately.
