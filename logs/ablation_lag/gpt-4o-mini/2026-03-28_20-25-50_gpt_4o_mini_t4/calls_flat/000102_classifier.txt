## Failure Mechanism

However, since there is no call to 'release' for the reserved inventory, the product remains reserved, leading to an inability to fulfill future orders for that product until the reservation is manually reset.

## Fix Strategy
By adding a call to the 'release' function in the exception handling block of 'place_order', the code ensures that any reserved inventory is properly released when a payment fails. This change directly addresses the issue of inventory being stuck as reserved.
