## Root Cause
The function charge() in payment.py returns early when a cached transaction exists, skipping the call to log_charge().

## Failure Mechanism
When charge() is called with a txn_id that is already in _charge_cache, it returns the cached result without executing log_charge(), resulting in missing audit log entries for cached charges.

## Broken Invariant
Every call to charge(), whether it results in a cache hit or a new charge, must be recorded in the audit log via log_charge().

## Fix Strategy
By removing the early return in charge() and ensuring log_charge() is called regardless of cache status, the code guarantees all charge attempts are logged, maintaining audit completeness.
