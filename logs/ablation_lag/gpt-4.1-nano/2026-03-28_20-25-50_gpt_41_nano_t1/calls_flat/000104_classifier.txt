## Failure Mechanism
If payment processing fails (raises ValueError), the reservation remains active and the audit entry remains, causing inventory to be reserved for an uncompleted order and the audit log to contain entries for failed transactions, leading to data inconsistency and potential stock mismanagement.

## Broken Invariant
The inventory reservation and audit log must be rolled back if payment processing fails during place_order, ensuring that reserved stock and audit entries only exist for successfully paid orders.

## Fix Strategy
Adding a try-except block around process() in place_order, and in the except clause, releasing the reserved inventory and removing the corresponding audit entry, restores consistency by ensuring that failed payment attempts do not leave residual reservations or audit entries.
