In service.py, the update_record function performs cache_invalidate(key) followed by cache_set(key, value), which is redundant because cache_set overwrites the cache entry without needing prior invalidation.

## Failure Mechanism
When update_record is called, it first invalidates the cache for the key, removing the cached value, and then sets the cache again with the new value. This unnecessary invalidation step causes a brief window where the cache is empty, which could lead to cache misses or race conditions if other processes access the cache in between.

## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy
Removing the cache_invalidate(key) call from update_record ensures that cache_set(key, value) directly updates the cache with the new value, eliminating the redundant invalidation step and maintaining cache consistency efficiently.
