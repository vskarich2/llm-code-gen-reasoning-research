## Failure Mechanism
When use_new_pricing is set to True in checkout(), create_invoice() is called without considering this flag, resulting in compute_price() always using the global 'new_pricing' flag, which may be False, thus ignoring the local override and not respecting the intended pricing version.

## Broken Invariant
The invoice's 'pricing_version' should reflect the value of use_new_pricing passed to checkout(), but currently it always reflects the global 'new_pricing' flag, ignoring the local override.

## Fix Strategy
Modify create_invoice() to accept a use_new_pricing parameter and pass it from checkout(), and update get_pricing_version() to accept this parameter, ensuring the invoice's 'pricing_version' accurately reflects the local override.
