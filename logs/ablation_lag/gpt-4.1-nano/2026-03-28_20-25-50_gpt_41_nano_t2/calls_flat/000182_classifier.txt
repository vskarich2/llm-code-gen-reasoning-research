## Failure Mechanism

## Broken Invariant

## Fix Strategy
Removing the call to freeze_view() from process_batch and process_incremental eliminates redundant view rebuilding, relying on stage() to set the view, thus simplifying the pipeline and maintaining view consistency without unnecessary recomputation.
