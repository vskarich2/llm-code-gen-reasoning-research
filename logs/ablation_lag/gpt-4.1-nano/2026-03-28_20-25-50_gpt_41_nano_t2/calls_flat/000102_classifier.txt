# Developer's Reasoning

## Root Cause
In the order_service.py, the place_order function reserves inventory with reserve(product_id, qty) but does not release the reservation if the payment process fails, specifically when _process_payment raises a ValueError. This leaves the _reserved count increased even though the payment was unsuccessful.

## Failure Mechanism
When a payment failure occurs during place_order, the exception is re-raised without releasing the inventory reservation, causing the reserved quantity to remain allocated to the order, thus leaving the inventory in a reserved state despite the payment not completing successfully.

## Broken Invariant
After a payment failure during place_order, the reserved inventory for the product should be released, ensuring that available stock reflects only successful reservations, and no inventory remains erroneously reserved after a failed payment.

## Fix Strategy
Adding a call to release(product_id, qty) inside the except block in place_order ensures that the reservation is canceled if payment fails, restoring the inventory's reserved count to its previous state and maintaining the invariant that reservations only exist for successful payments.
