# Developer's Reasoning

## Root Cause
The client.py module captures the value of get_config() at import time into the variable _client_config, which is initialized only once. Therefore, subsequent calls to reset_config() do not update _client_config, causing the client to use stale configuration values.

## Failure Mechanism
After calling reset_config() to clear the cached configuration, get_config() reloads the new configuration, but _client_config in client.py remains unchanged. As a result, get_db_url() and get_timeout() return outdated values, such as the old timeout, despite the reset.

## Broken Invariant
The variables get_db_url() and get_timeout() should always reflect the current configuration returned by get_config(), even after reset_config() is called.

## Fix Strategy
By removing the eager capture of get_config() into _client_config and instead calling get_config() within get_db_url() and get_timeout(), the client will always retrieve the latest configuration, including after reset_config().
