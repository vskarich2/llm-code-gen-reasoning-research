## Root Cause
In pipeline.ingest (code_snippets_v2/retry_dup_c/pipeline.py) the outer retry loop does not stop after a successful send because there is no 'break' after calling send_with_retry. This causes the pipeline to call send_with_retry multiple times for the same message. The causal relationship: pipeline.ingest always iterates max_pipeline_retries times, and each successful call to send_with_retry results in append(msg) via sender.send -> store.append, so multiple iterations produce multiple stored messages.

## Failure Mechanism
Concrete scenario: ingest(msg, max_pipeline_retries=2, fail_first=True) calls send_with_retry(msg, max_retries=2, fail_first=True). Inside send_with_retry, sender.send is attempted; the first sender.send call increments sender._attempt_count to 1 and raises (transient failure). send_with_retry retries and the second sender.send increments _attempt_count to 2 and succeeds, causing store.append(msg) and store.notify(msg) once. Because pipeline.ingest does not break, the outer loop runs a second iteration and calls send_with_retry again. That second outer iteration causes sender.send to be invoked again (incrementing _attempt_count further) and succeed, causing a second append of the same message. Combined with other configurations of max_retries and pipeline retries, this can lead to messages appearing 3–4 times. The key variables involved are pipeline.max_pipeline_retries, sender._attempt_count and store._messages (via append), and the missing break causes repeated successful calls that append duplicates.

## Broken Invariant
After ingest(msg, ... ) returns True, the store must contain at most one appended copy of that msg per logical ingest call. Currently, ingest can append the same msg multiple times in one call (e.g., when fail_first=True and max_pipeline_retries>1), violating the invariant 'a successful ingest results in a single store.append for that message'.

## Fix Strategy
Stop the outer retry loop as soon as send_with_retry succeeds by adding a break after the call in pipeline.ingest. This ensures that once the sender has completed a successful send (and append), the pipeline will not invoke the sender again for the same message. Additionally, fix reset() in pipeline to invoke the module-level reset helpers (store.reset and reset_sender) instead of trying to directly reassign variables that belong to other modules; this prevents incorrect cross-module state mutation and avoids NameError while resetting the test environment.
