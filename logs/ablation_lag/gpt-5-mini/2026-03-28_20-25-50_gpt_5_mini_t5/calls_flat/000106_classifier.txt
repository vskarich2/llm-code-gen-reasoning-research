## Root Cause
In order_service.place_order the code calls reserve(product_id, qty) and add_audit_entry(...) but when payment.process raises ValueError the except block simply re-raises without calling release(product_id, qty) or remove_audit_entry(order_id). As a result the inventory module's _reserved for that product (modified by reserve) and the payment module's _audit_log (modified by add_audit_entry) are left mutated when payment.process fails.

## Failure Mechanism
Concrete scenario: payment.set_gateway_fail(True) causes payment.process(...) to raise ValueError. place_order reserves qty via inventory.reserve (incrementing inventory._reserved[product_id]) and appends an audit entry via payment.add_audit_entry (appending into payment._audit_log). process then raises; place_order's except clause re-raises without executing any cleanup. The net effect is that inventory._reserved still includes the order's qty and payment._audit_log still contains the order entry even though the order failed — producing a persistent, inconsistent state across inventory and audit logs.

## Broken Invariant
After place_order raises ValueError for an order (i.e. payment failed), the system invariant must hold that inventory.get_reserved(product_id) equals its pre-order reserved count and payment.get_audit_log() must not contain an entry with that order's order_id. Currently, a failed place_order leaves inventory.get_reserved(product_id) increased by qty and payment.get_audit_log() containing the order_id.

## Fix Strategy
On payment failure, perform compensating actions that exactly undo the earlier side-effects. I added an except handler in place_order that calls release(product_id, qty) to decrement inventory._reserved and remove_audit_entry(order_id) to remove the audit entry, then re-raises the original ValueError. I also fixed reset() to call inventory.reset() and payment.reset() so tests won't observe stale cross-module state. These changes ensure the reserve and audit entry are reverted if process fails, restoring the promised invariant.
