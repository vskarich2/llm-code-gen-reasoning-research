This leads to both withdrawals being approved under interleaving, causing the balance to go negative. Specifically, the variable result['approved'] is set based on the balance at check time, but between the check and the act, the balance can change due to other concurrent withdrawals, leading to inconsistent state.

Fix Strategy:
Modify make_withdraw_steps to perform the check and withdrawal atomically by combining the check and do_withdraw operations into a single step that verifies the balance and updates it in one operation. Replace the separate check and act steps with a single step that first checks the balance, then performs the withdrawal only if sufficient funds remain, thus preventing overdraft even under interleaving.
