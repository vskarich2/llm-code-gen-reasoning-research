This means 'config' references the same dictionary object as DEFAULTS, so any modifications to 'config' (such as update) will also modify DEFAULTS, violating the intended immutability of the defaults.

## Failure Mechanism

By replacing 'config = DEFAULTS' with 'config = DEFAULTS.copy()', create_config now creates a shallow copy of DEFAULTS, ensuring modifications to 'config' do not affect DEFAULTS. This preserves the original defaults and prevents side effects when overrides are applied.
